Privacy Notice

A plain-English summary of what we collect, why, and how to have it removed.

Britten Woodworking is a one-person custom woodworking business based in Connecticut. This page explains how information submitted through brittenwoodworking.com is handled.

What We Collect

When you submit a contact form, we receive the information you choose to provide: your first name, last name, email address, phone number (if you choose to share it), and the message you write. Our forms are processed by Netlify, our hosting provider, which also records the IP address and timestamp of each submission as part of standard security and abuse prevention.

We do not run analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies on this site. We do not collect information about you when you simply browse the portfolio — only when you actively submit a form.

Why We Collect It

We use the information you submit to reply to your inquiry, discuss your project, and provide a quote. That is the only purpose. We do not send marketing emails, share your information with other businesses, or sell it to anyone.

Where It Goes

Form submissions are stored in our Netlify Forms dashboard and forwarded to Michael Britten’s email at michaelspikebritten@gmail.com, which is hosted by Google. Both providers have their own privacy practices, which apply to the data they hold on our behalf.

The third parties that receive your submission are limited to:

  • Netlify — hosting and form processing
  • Google — email delivery (Gmail) and font hosting (Google Fonts)

How Long We Keep It

We retain contact form submissions while a project is being discussed and for a reasonable period afterward in case follow-up questions arise. You can request deletion at any time — see below.

Your Choices

To request a copy of any information we hold about you, or to ask that it be deleted, email michaelspikebritten@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy Request”. We’ll confirm and complete the request within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to This Notice

If we materially change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and revise the date below.
